About

I am a political science researcher with hands-on experience in development projects. I have designed, executed, and evaluated a rural development program using a range of qualitative and quantitative tools — including 75+ interviews, 20 focus group discussions, and baseline and endline surveys — and have produced project reports built on that analysis.

As a political consultant, I have worked extensively with electoral rolls, Form 20 data, and official sources such as NITI Aayog, NFHS, and WDI, cleaning and structuring this data for constituency profiling and deeper research. Over the last three years, I've developed a strong focus on the electoral behavior of the Indian electorate — one of the largest and most dynamic in the world — and I write on this alongside a broader interest in the gender dimensions of political life.

I try to understand concepts and systems through their intersectionality, and my interest currently lies at the intersection of politics, media, and psychology. I'm building my body of work around how these three forces interact with and shape one another.
